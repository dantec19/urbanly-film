# Before You Commit — storyboard

Silent product film for Urbanly / CityCompass. 1920×1080, 60 fps, 63 s (the brief: about one minute). Landing page and client pitches.

## The idea

A real city (Buenos Aires, from the committed region binaries) is drawn from a single point, stands up
in 3D, fills with agents, and lives out its next decade. The decade stacks up as a tower of yearly map
tiles. A planner draws a line; the network answers; eight indicators read the same city. A second line
gives a second tower beside the first, both standing on the same present. The two towers and their
shared base fold into the Urbanly voxel U: the mark was always two futures on one foundation, with the
tops (the last year) lifted off.

## Colour: one meaning each

| colour | token | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| obsidian plum `#1B1418` → `#281E24` | ground | the night table the model sits on |
| bone `#F0F4EF` | the city as it is | streets, footprints, existing buildings |
| amber `#FFB547` | life | agents (people, households, firms, developers) and what they build |
| coral `#F77138` | your decision | the line you draw, the zoning envelope, the edit |
| viridis `#440154 → #FDE725` | measurement | travel time, the eight indicators |
| teal `#92ADAE` | the readout | labels, eyebrows, numbers |

Type: Montserrat 300 headlines (tight tracking), Archivo uppercase labels (.11em) and all numbers
(tabular). Left-aligned. No emoji, no icons, no gradients for decoration.

## Motion rules

- One world, one continuous camera: no hard cuts. Every scene hands over by a camera move or a
  match (a colour field becomes a layer, a line becomes a fork, towers become the mark).
- Frames are pure functions of t. Every change eases; reveals stagger; buildings overshoot and settle.
- One read at a time. Headlines get ≥ 2.5 s on screen after they finish appearing.

## Shots (times in seconds)

S1 Origin 0–9.5 [in: from obsidian]
- 0.0–0.8 a warm point of light at Plaza de Mayo, a slow breath (anticipation)
- 0.8–4.6 the real street network grows from it in network order, hot tips cooling to bone; camera
  pulls back from 0.7 km to the whole city (~22 km); the river stays a dark void
- 4.4–7.4 label: BUENOS AIRES + real counts (streets km, parcels, buildings)
- 5.4–9.5 tilt to axonometric; buildings rise in a wave from the Plaza outward, real heights

S2 Everyone in it 9.5–17 [camera dives into a neighbourhood; dusk falls]
- windows light amber; thousands of amber agents stream along real streets toward job centres
- headline builds word by word, each word tagged in the scene:
  "Every person, household, firm and developer is an agent."

S3 What pencils out 17–25 [follow the developer glyph to one parcel]
- coral dashed zoning envelope over an old low house
- receipt card prints: land, cost, sales, margin; first try fails (▼), second passes (▲)
- the old house sinks, the new building rises floor by floor in amber
- headline: "Developers build only what pencils out."

S4 The decade 25–33 [pull out to the city]
- year counter YEAR 1 → YEAR 10 (the run is 2022–2031; relative years); the model's real developments
  rise in amber, year by year
- the decade stacks into a tower of yearly tiles (space-time stack), growth glowing on its year
- headline: "The next ten years, one year at a time."

S5 Draw a line 33–40.5 [tower settles back to the present, top-down]
- a coral line is drawn across the city; stations pop
- walk-time wavefronts run along the real streets (viridis); readout: homes newly within a 10-minute
  walk of the Subte
- headline: "Draw a line. The network answers."

S6 The compass 40.5–46.5
- an eight-point compass rose opens over one station's neighbourhood: each wedge shows one reading
  (land value, flat prices, room to build, built density, homes per hectare, trees, walk to the Subte,
  new homes next decade)
- headline: "One city, read eight ways."

S7 Two futures 46.5–53.5 [the coral line forks; camera to isometric]
- the present tile at the bottom; two towers grow from it side by side: today's plan, and with the line
  (a second real run, same seed, with the line plus 38 m zoning within 500 m of its stations: in the
  Buenos Aires model the line alone changes nothing developers see)
- readouts diverge under each tower: new homes within 800 m of the 13 stations, 6,641 vs 9,120 (+37%)
- headline: "Put one future beside another."

S8 The mark 53.5–63 [out: to obsidian]
- the two towers and their shared base fold into the voxel U; the YEAR 10 tiles lift off as the two tiles
- URBANLY wordmark; "Watch your city's next decade before you commit to it."; urbanly.org
- hold ≥ 3 s; fade

Rhyme: the film opens on one point of light becoming a city, and closes on the city's two futures
becoming one mark.
